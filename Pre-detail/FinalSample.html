My Hobbies

Here is a heirarchy list of what I do most often in my free time:

  1. Reading - mostly online guides for my other hobbies and whatever book I deem worthy from my collection (probably another Brandon Mull book or a random book I picked up in a book sale)
  2. Video Games - a variety of genres with my goal now being One For Each, ranging from MOBAs, FPS, 3D Fighting Games, Platformers, Tactics or TRPGs, and even some Puzzle Games
  3. Pet Keeping — tending my new brothers and sisters in the form of cats aptly named after our family's favorite fable author - Beatrix Potter (as the mother cat), and the characters in her book (as her children); Flopsy, Mopsy, Cottontail, and Peter.
  4. Drawing — experimenting with new ideas and techniques, doodling on my notes, and generally going through pads of paper just to get a cool scene or character out of my head.
