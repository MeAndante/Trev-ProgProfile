History

The College of Information Technology Education (CITE) was established to respond to the growing demand for specialized IT education and to prepare graduates for the dynamic digital landscape. It traces its roots to the College of Arts & Sciences (CAS), which initially offered foundational courses in computing and information technology.

On March 14, 2025, the Board of Regents formally approved the establishment of CITE as a separate college, marking a significant milestone in the university's commitment to advancing technology education. The creation of CITE was the result of the collaborative initiatives of three Deans of CAS—Dr. Filemon A. Pamittan, Dr. Lori Shayne A. Busa, and Dr. Julius S. Valderama. The proposal for the establishment of CITE was presented during the term of Dr. Valderama, the current Dean of CAS, to the University Academic Council and subsequently to the Board of Regents. This milestone was realized through the visionary leadership of University President Dr. Wilfredo A. Dumale, Jr., whose unwavering support for academic excellence and institutional development ensured the smooth establishment and formal recognition of the college. The initiative enabled a more focused curriculum, the expansion of research initiatives, and the creation of opportunities for innovation and collaboration in the IT sector. Prior to the establishment of CITE, the academic programs BSCS, BSIT, and BSIS were offered under CAS; their transfer to the new college allowed for specialized focus and growth in the field of information technology.

CITE currently offers three academic programs: Bachelor of Science in Computer Science (BSCS), Bachelor of Science in Information Technology (BSIT), and Bachelor of Science in Information Systems (BSIS). For the first semester of the academic year 2025-2026, the college has a total enrollment of 1,275 students, reflecting its growing reputation and relevance.

Under the leadership of its first dean, Dr. Carmelo Alejo D. Bisquera, and with the continued support of the university administration, CITE continues to pursue excellence in teaching, research, and community engagement, preparing graduates to contribute meaningfully to the global IT community and the advancement of technology-driven solutions.

Vision, Mission, Goals, and Objectives

VISION

To be a leading college that develops skilled, responsible and innovative IT Professionals for a better digital future.

MISSION

The College of Information Technology Education aims to empower students with the skills and mindset needed to succeed in the dynamic IT industry.

GOALS

To provide a board IT education that prepares students for successful careers in various sectors of the technology industry.

  1. To instill in students the importance of acting with integrity and contributing positively to the community.
  2. To promote groundbreaking IT research aimed at global challenges.
  3. To build strong connections with business and past graduates, which helps us improve academic programs and job placement opportunities.
  4. To provide an environment that sparks critical thinking, creativity, and a commitment to lifelong learning.

OBJECTIVES

  1. Academic Excellence: Offer IT programs that are accredited and up to date as well as micro-credentials that adhere to global standards of quality and align with the needs of the technology industry.
  2. Skills Development: Empower students with practical, industry-relevant skills through hands-on projects, pitching, internships, and collaboration with industry professionals.
  3. Ethical Leadership: Cultivate an environment that promotes ethical choices, responsibility, and integrity in how technology is used and developed.
  4. Global Competitiveness: Equip students for the global IT job market by building up their communication, problem solving, and teamwork abilities.
  5. Research and Innovation: Foster an environment of research and tech innovation that helps our local community.

Degree Programs

Explore our academic programs designed to develop technical competence, critical thinking, and industry-relevant skills for success in the digital field.

BS in Computer Science

This program includes the study of computing concepts and theories, algorithmic foundations and new developments in computing. It prepares students to design and create algorithmically complex software and develop new and effective algorithms for solving computing problems.

BS in Information Technology

This program includes the study of the utilization of both hardware and software technologies involving planning, installing, customizing, operating, managing and administering, and maintaining information infrastructure that provides computing solutions to address the needs of an organization.

BS in Information Systems

This program includes the study of application and effect of information technology to organizations. It also prepare graduates to implement an information systems, which considers complex technological and organizational factors affecting it.
